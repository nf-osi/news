## Do you want to advance the field of neurofibromatosis (NF) and rare diseases? Hack for NF, and be part of developing solutions to end rare diseases!

![Hack for NF 2019](https://news.nfdataportal.org/wp-content/uploads/2020/09/2019hackathon-scaled.jpg) Hackers present rare disease solutions at the 2019 NF Hackathon in San Francisco, CA. Image courtesy of Children's Tumor Foundation.

Rare diseases affect nearly 350 million people around the world, and NF, specifically, affects 1 in 3,000 people. Hack for NF 2020 — hosted by the [Children's Tumor Foundation (CTF)](https://www.ctf.org/) and sponsored by numerous organizations and data partners, including [Sage Bionetworks](https://sagebionetworks.org/) and the [NF Data Portal](https://nf.synapse.org) — will **bring together patients, scientists, researchers, clinicians, startups, developers, data analysts, and other hackathon enthusiasts to analyze datasets and create solutions** ranging from new research methods, data visualization, and patient engagement.

**We need your help to end NF and other rare diseases.** Hacker participants will compete to create a data-driven framework, promote reusable strategies, and incorporate emerging technologies such as AI, ML, or NLP to find new insights, refine algorithms, and identify alternative therapies and research. Three hackathon teams will win a $7,500 prize, as well as the opportunity to win additional grant monies and participate in the NF Virtual Institute and NF Incubator. This year's hackathon will be virtual, and will run from October 2 - November 13.
