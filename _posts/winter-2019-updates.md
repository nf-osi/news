**2nd NF Hackathon results**

The NF-OSI ran the 2nd ever NF Hackathon in San Francisco this past September. During this collaborative scientific event, participants explored genomic, drug screening, imaging, and other data for NF1 and NF2, and presented their findings at the Annual NF Conference. You can see a wrap up of the [event](https://sv.ai/neurofibromatosis), including [project summaries](https://github.com/Sage-Bionetworks/nf-hackathon-2019/blob/master/README.md#links-to-projects), [the data used](https://www.synapse.org/#!Synapse:syn18666641/wiki/), and a [Twitter summary](https://twitter.com/i/events/1173995234071498755). Thanks to all of the participants, [mentors, and organizers](https://sv.ai/neurofibromatosis-people)!

**Paper highlight: Engaging a community to enable disease-centric data sharing with the NF Data Portal**

Members of the NF-OSI recently published a commentary in the journal _Scientific Data,_ describing the current approach we are taking to data sharing, the community of stakeholders in this initiative, and our plans for the future. [Read the commentary](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41597-019-0317-x).

**Funding opportunities: CTF Drug Discovery Initiative Registered Reports (DDI-RR) and Young Investigator Awards (YIA)**

The Children’s Tumor Foundation is currently accepting letters of intent for two grant opportunities:

-   The **DDI-RR program** is a funder-publisher collaboration that promotes accelerated, reproducible, and rigorous publishing of scientific results. LOIs are due December 31, 2019.
-   **CTF YIA awards** provide two-year support to PhD students and postdoctoral researchers studying NF1, NF2, and schwannomatosis. LOIs are due January 3, 2020. More information about both grants can be found on [the CTF website](https://www.ctf.org/research/awards-applications).
