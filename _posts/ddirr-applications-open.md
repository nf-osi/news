[Applications](https://www.ctf.org/research/drug-discovery-initiative-registered-reports-ddirr#application) are now open for the [Children's Tumor Foundation](https://www.ctf.org/)'s (CTF) 2021 cycle of [Drug Discovery Initiative Registered Reports (DDIRR) award program](https://www.ctf.org/research/drug-discovery-initiative-registered-reports-ddirr#application), a program to fund early stage testing of candidate drug therapies for neurofibromatosis treatment. This program is guided by the [Registered Reports](https://www.cos.io/initiatives/registered-reports) model, which involves pre-results peer review to align on scientific values and practices and encourages rigorous and reproducible science.

To facilitate this model, CTF and the publisher _PLOS ONE_ have partnered together. DDIRR's first output article, titled ["Evaluating modified diets and dietary supplement therapies for reducing muscle lipid accumulation and improving muscle function in neurofibromatosis type 1 (NF1)"](https://journals.plos.org/plosone/article/comments?id=10.1371/journal.pone.0237097), was just published _PLOS ONE_ in August 2020.

Letters of intent to apply for DDIRR are due December 14, 2020.
